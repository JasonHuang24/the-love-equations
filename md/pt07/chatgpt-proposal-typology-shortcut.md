# PROPOSAL P2 - The Typology Shortcut

**Subject:** A folk typology becomes a screening instrument when a category is promoted from conversation or self-description to a proxy for pair-specific compatibility. The shortcut can cheaply organize attention, but it replaces person-level evidence with a type-to-outcome rule. Use a label to open questions; use observed values, behavior, responsiveness, and interaction to answer them.

**Parent entry:** `frameworks:interaction-gate` (**The Interaction Gate**). The parent permits pre-contact evidence to reject a known mismatch while reserving positive compatibility for richer interaction. This child asks whether the proposed mismatch is actually known or merely inferred from a folk category. `frameworks:meeting-channel` is related because app filters change the screening order. `frameworks:diagnostic-turn` is an explicit boundary owner for pop-clinical labels rather than astrology, MBTI, love languages, blood types, and similar folk types.

## Sourced tiered claims

1. **Tier 3 - observed product and cultural use:** Bumble exposed zodiac sign as a profile filter, while a Korea Times essay reports app-prioritized MBTI matching and distinguishes playful type talk from weeding out potential partners. These sources document live category-based screening and its own internal boundary; they provide no prevalence or outcome estimate. [Allure on Bumble's zodiac filter](https://www.allure.com/story/bumble-dating-app-astrology-sign-filter); [Korea Times on MBTI](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/opinion/20230627/obsession-with-mbti).
2. **Tier 1 - observational primary evidence:** Chopik et al. examined love-language matching with multiple analytic approaches in 954 male/female established couples in mid and later life. Their published abstract reports virtually no additional outcome variation from matching beyond individual and partner personality traits and attachment orientations, and questions whether the categories are distinct from broader responsiveness. Mostova, Stolarski, and Matthews examined 100 sexually active heterosexual couples and found that smaller continuous item-level discrepancies between preferred and partner-expressed behaviors were associated with relationship and sexual satisfaction. That measure spans all five domains and may capture responsive behavior rather than a primary type; the authors acknowledge reverse causation and unmeasured confounding. Neither study tests category-based screening in forming pairs. DOI [`10.1093/geroni/igaf122.1395`](https://doi.org/10.1093/geroni/igaf122.1395); DOI [`10.1371/journal.pone.0269429`](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0269429).
3. **Tier 2 - independent research review:** Pittenger's review of MBTI theory, psychometrics, experiments, applications, and ethics concluded that the available literature supplied insufficient evidence for the test's tenets and utility claims. That conclusion is broader than romance and does not by itself estimate dating compatibility, but it weakens the move from a four-letter type to a predictive screen. DOI [`10.3102/00346543063004467`](https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543063004467).
4. **Tier 1 - existing parent evidence:** Joel, Eastwick, and Finkel tested whether a large set of pre-interaction traits and preferences could predict pair-specific romantic desire in speed-dating data. The current Interaction Gate correctly owns the result: pre-contact variables did not reliably rank who would uniquely desire whom, although general desirability was more predictable. This supports reserving positive compatibility for interaction rather than a profile type. DOI [`10.1177/0956797617714580`](https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797617714580).

## Common misreadings

- Matching folk types predicts easier communication and lasting harmony for prospective partners.
- An incompatible zodiac sign reveals how a partner will behave after commitment.
- Shared love language labels forecast pair compatibility better than attentive responsiveness does.

## Boundaries

- A folk type can be an icebreaker, reflective vocabulary, ritual, identity expression, or prompt for direct questions without acting as a compatibility score.
- A stated preference or behavior may matter even when the category system containing it lacks predictive validity. Responding to an expressed need differs from matching type labels.
- A label can reveal a real value mismatch about the importance of astrology, personality systems, or relationship rituals. That mismatch belongs to the expressed value, not a chart's predicted pairing.
- A profile filter changes who receives consideration; it does not show that the excluded category would have produced worse interaction or outcomes.
- The astrology evidence concerns a sun-sign product filter and its discourse. It neither tests full natal practice nor rules on astrology as a source of personal meaning.
- The MBTI review concerns evidence for test tenets and utility across uses. It does not estimate a romantic effect and does not make every four-letter conversation harmful.
- The MBTI review was published in 1993 and evaluates the literature and instrument claims then available; it does not evaluate every later revision or proprietary implementation.
- The love-language finding concerns matching in established male/female couples in mid and later life. It does not test every preference measure, newly forming pair, or responsive behavior.
- Continuous personality traits, shared values, and observed interaction can carry information even when a categorical folk type is a poor proxy.
- The scout sources do not estimate whether repeated label use changes expectations or behavior through a self-fulfilling process.
- Pop-clinical attachment, trauma, narcissism, and therapy-speak labels remain owned by `frameworks:diagnostic-turn`.

## Deliberate nonclaims

- No typology is declared wholly useless for reflection, conversation, community, or ritual.
- No user population, country, platform, or age group is assigned a prevalence of type-based screening.
- No category pair is assigned a relationship success rate or ranked compatibility score.
- No claim says personality similarity, shared values, or preference responsiveness never matter.
- No national explanation is drawn from the Korea Times author's claims about collectivism, competition, or social media.
- No full astrological system is equated with a single zodiac sign.
- No folk label is treated as a clinical diagnosis.

## Proposed standard

Before using a folk type to rank or delete a candidate, state the person-level claim the type is supposed to predict and ask whether evidence validates that prediction in the relevant relationship stage. Keep the type as a prompt when it helps conversation. Treat it as a mismatch only when direct values or observed behavior establish the mismatch; reserve positive compatibility for reciprocal interaction.
